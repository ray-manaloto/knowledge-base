# Q: Is a fresher re-extraction automatically better than the chunk it replaces?

## Answer

No. Two of six chunks were REGRESSIONS against what they superseded (workflows 32 vs 40, goal 22 vs 24), and a successful merge would have shipped both silently because graphify reports nodes replaced but never whether the replacement is better. Compare node counts against THE CHUNK YOU ACTUALLY SUPERSEDE - not the richest chunk for that page under a different identity, since a 152-line page cannot honestly yield the 54 nodes a multi-source research chunk produced. Count is a floor, not a quality measure. Keeping every page above its bar also makes the batch net positive, so the count-based shrink guard structurally cannot fire.
