How wide should the guard go

Codex has 28 commands. I wrote the guard for two of them.

A hole I made

One safe-looking word lets the rest through. I let a command skip the guard if any part of it looks harmless — so pairing a harmless word with a costly one gets past. That is my mistake, not a design choice, and I will fix it either way.

The worst gap

One command edits your working files directly, and nothing anywhere stops it. Not this guard, not any other, and not in your other repo either.

The widest gap

One command runs whatever you hand it. Everything we have built to catch bad commands can be walked around by going through that one.

The quiet one

Some commands delete saved sessions, and we already lose a day of them every day. Not urgent, but it is the kind of loss nobody notices until it is asked for.

What I need from you: tell me how far to take it — the money ones only, or the file-and-command ones too.
