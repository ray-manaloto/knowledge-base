graphify · openai-cli · measured 2026-09-02

Asking for the other engine gives you the old answer back.

You wanted to re-run already-extracted sources on a different AI backend and compare. Right now that command silently does nothing — and nothing the graph keeps would tell you.

Switching
Already built — the fork can fall back to a second engine mid-run. That part works.
Proving it
Missing where it counts. Nothing the graph keeps says which engine produced which piece of data. Some throwaway local logs do — they are not kept, not shared, and not what anyone would read.
Worse
Falling back mixes two engines into one graph on purpose, and marks nothing. Any mixing that already happened is now invisible.
Fix
Two changes to our own copy — put the engine in the lookup key, stamp it on what gets saved. Small to describe; the plan to land them safely runs to 21 steps.

→ One choice, open since 26 August.

Separate the saved work per engine. Correct and simple. Costs re-doing the work once for each engine — the exact double-spend the saving exists to avoid.

Keep sharing it, but label who made it. Cheapest. Makes the reuse visible instead of preventing it — a run can then say “N of these came from the other engine.”

Share by default, separate on request. Most flexible, most moving parts.

Corrected after publication: this was written as a new finding. It is not — it was measured and filed a week ago, and has been waiting on this choice since.
