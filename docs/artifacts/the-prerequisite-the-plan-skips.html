The Prerequisite the Plan Skips

The tracked chain and task_plan.md are not competing for “what’s next.” They are the same road, and the plan starts one step down it.

Why it looked like a conflict

Two files, two scopes, one road

The chain file is a tracker backlog — 11 [[ticket]] entries ordered by blockers, and #638 is the head. task_plan.md is intra-round working state for one specific job: turning five design artifacts into a spec.

They name different next steps because they are pointing at different points on the same line. Nothing is competing. The chain file says so in its own comment, above the #638 entry:

“It sits ahead of #581 because the whole upgrade-protocol design rests on that session’s facts, several of which were CORRECTED mid-session — so the review is a prerequisite for trusting any of them, not a parallel nicety.”

And #638 §10 line 137 states the outcome it expects:

“a findings list that probably triggers another /grilling round before /to-spec.”

Plan Phase 3 is /to-spec. So the re-grill belongs before Phase 2, because the re-grill is what decides whether the five questions are even asking the right things.

Correction to what I told you

I asked you to pick. That was the wrong question.

My resume report ended “Want me to start on the #638 re-grill, or on plan Phase 2’s five questions?” — an either/or. It is neither: it is re-grill, then Phase 2, and the plan’s pointer is what made them look like alternatives.

The session-resume skill’s rule (“a plan is intra-round working state; the plan loses”) landed on the right ordering here by luck, not by reasoning — it framed a dependency as an authority contest. The rule is built for a plan left over from a finished round. This plan was deliberately kept live across the boundary (ledger tick 20: “plan stays LIVE (1/5 phases) so it is deliberately not archived”), which is the case the rule does not cover.

The fix

One line in the plan, not a re-plan

Phase 2 is missing its first checkbox. The repair is to insert the re-grill ahead of Q1 and move ## Next Step onto it — after which the chain and the plan read the same sentence, and this page stops being needed.
