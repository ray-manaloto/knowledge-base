corrected after publishing

The first version of this page said the newer engine was switched off. That was wrong, and it was wrong in an instructive way — kept here rather than quietly replaced, in case it was already acted on.

two questions that look identical

The switch is off. The new engine is running anyway.

what I asked

“Is the switch on?”

Answer: no. True, checked twice, from two places. And it settles nothing.

what mattered

“Which engine runs?”

Answer: the new one. The model you use asks for it by name, and that beats the switch.

why the check couldn’t have caught it

The command I ran reports switches. It never looks at which model you picked — and that is what actually decides.

I ran it from two different places to prove it wasn’t lying. It wasn’t. It was answering a different question, correctly.

Our own notes said so all along: “Sol and Terra already select v2 — do not turn the switch on for them.”

So: leave the switch alone. Turning it on changes nothing here and would quietly alter how four other models behave. I need nothing from you on this one.
