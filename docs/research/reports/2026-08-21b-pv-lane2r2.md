Control-armed on the pinned ruff 0.16.3: **both** `except (Exception, SystemExit):` and `except Exception, SystemExit:` emit `BLE001 Do not catch blind exception: Exception`; `except BaseException:` too. Ray forbids new suppression → §3 as written makes lint rc 1. Three clean routes, all armed: (i) a **named union** carrying no `Exception`/`BaseException` — `(ValueError, TypeError, LookupError, OSError, ImportError, RuntimeError, SystemExit)` produced zero BLE001, and `ImportError` covers `PackageNotFoundError`, `TypeError` covers the sdist case; (ii) `logging.exception(...)` inside the handler exempts BLE001 — but this module has no logger and uses `print` as its UI (T201 ignored), so that is a design change; (iii) re-raise exempts it (inapplicable — the handler must not raise). **Recommend (i)**; the "property, not two types" comment survives either way. The d8114ab1 planner has **zero** BLE001 today, so this would be the file's first.
