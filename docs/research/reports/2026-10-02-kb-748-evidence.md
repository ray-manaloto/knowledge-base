# #748 evidence: kb-serve's probe is bounded by CPU work, not wall clock

The follow-on to #838 (`2026-10-02-kb-838-evidence.md`). That fix grouped the
real-graph tests and moved the eval canary to a CPU-time bound. It left kb-serve's
120 s wall bound, and named that as the residual. The residual then fired.

## The failure (inherited from #748's comment, not re-measured here)

`test_kb_serve_actually_answers_mcp@real_graph` failed twice in the 000-KB
kb-ship with "timed out waiting for reply id=1". Load was 8.8 -> 57 in one run
and 27 -> 47 in the other. A paired single-test probe passed on main @ 683463b5
in 35.0 s and on the branch in 28.9 s.

## Measured this round (2026-10-02, 756 MB graph)

| arm | wall | result |
|---|---|---|
| unthrottled, new bound (cost measurement) | 30.0 s | init, 10 tools; group CPU ~28.4 s |
| SIGSTOP starvation, requested duty 0.06, old `timeout=120` | 120.1 s | **FAIL**: timed out waiting for reply id=1 |
| same starvation, new `CpuBound(180)` | 154.7 s | **PASS**: init, 10 tools; group CPU ~30.4 s |

Load at start was 15.6, 16.2 and 5.7 respectively.

"Group CPU" is the sum over the probe's process group (mise + uv +
graphify-mcp), sampled every 0.5 s by the arm script. Requested duty is the
throttle's setting. The delivered share is higher, because #838's round-2 review
found the throttle gives its target extra run time. That does not change the
direction of the result.

## The change

- `evals.CpuWatch` factors out #838's CPU-budget and stall rules and its
  zombie guard. `run_command_cpu_bounded` uses it, and so does
  `mcp_probe.probe(..., bound=CpuBound(...))`. A wedged server (under 1 CPU-s
  per 60 s) or a runaway (over budget) still fails. A busy host does not.
- The live arm uses `bound=CpuBound(180)`, ~6x the measured cost, the same
  ratio as the canary's.
- Tests on fake servers, with no graph needed:
  - a wedge fails as "stalled"
  - a runaway fails on its budget
  - a clean exit under a slow `ps` reports rc=0, not a stall
  - a starved server answers where a 3 s wall bound fails (its control)
- The wiring test is armed: reverting the live arm to `timeout=120` gives
  rc 1, and restoring it gives rc 0.
