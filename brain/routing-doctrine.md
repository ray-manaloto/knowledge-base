# routing-doctrine

The static routing table the vault is ADVISORY over. Route by where JUDGMENT lives, not task size.

- Correctness-critical ([[task-class-migration]], [[task-class-concurrency]], [[task-class-auth-security]]) to [[lane-codex]], high effort.
- Broad / mechanical ([[task-class-crud-boilerplate]], [[task-class-test-generation]]) to [[lane-antigravity]], medium-high.
- Live-web [[task-class-research]] to [[lane-grok]]; [[lane-claude-fallback]] is terminal.
Prefer a cheaper lane at higher effort over an expensive one at low effort.
A vault hint counts only at >=3 consistent outcomes in a task-class x lane cell — see [[how-hints-work]].
