# l-clarify-before-acting

Ask clarifying questions on ambiguous, multi-path, or hard-to-reverse work, leading with a recommended option.
Proceed directly on clear, low-risk, reversible tasks so clarification itself does not become needless delay.
On 2026-06-29, confirming a pivot avoided silently substituting an outer wrapper for impossible per-step hk timeouts.
Under [[delegation-discipline]], re-confirm any infeasible chosen approach with evidence before changing the specification.
