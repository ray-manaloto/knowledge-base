# l-debate-rounds-diminish-after-5

Focused debates usually capture more than 90 percent of their value within five rounds.
In the ten-round no-vscode review, rounds 1-5 found the gaps and architecture; later rounds added edge cases and confidence only.
Use [[delegation-discipline]] to cap focused reviews at five rounds while reserving longer runs for broad, multi-stage architecture.
When independent providers converge early, route remaining uncertainty through targeted [[cross-family-review]] instead of automatic rounds.
