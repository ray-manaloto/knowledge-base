# l-adversarial-review-convention

The 2026-04-10b review converged from seven HIGH findings to SHIP across six Codex and Gemini rounds.
Keep the plan and review artifacts file-based, stage Gemini-readable copies outside excluded paths, and archive each round.
Prompts must require source introspection, file-and-line receipts, and explicit UNVERIFIED labels for unchecked claims.
Direct CLI invocation matters: heredoc stdin hung, while short file-referencing prompts worked reliably.
Use [[cross-family-review]] until both reviewers ship or only LOW observations remain.
